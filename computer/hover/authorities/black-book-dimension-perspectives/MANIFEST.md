## Claims currently derived from this authority

- Movement: Movement is Energy; its microcosmic expression is Individuality;
  its basic component is the Magnetic Monopole.
- Evolution: Evolution is Gravity; its microcosmic expression is the Mind;
  its basic component is the Personality Crystal.
- Being: Being is Matter; its microcosmic expression is the Body; its basic
  component is the atom in the dimension table and the Design Crystal in the
  crystal/monopole presentation.
- Design: Design is Structure; its microcosmic expression is the Ego; its
  basic component is the Design Crystal.
- Space: Space is Form; its microcosmic expression is Personality/Presence;
  it is described as a condition resulting from the interaction of the other
  four dimensional fields rather than a contributing component in the shown
  formula.
- The supplied Being perspective orders the five dimensions as Being,
  Movement, Space, Design, Evolution. This is retained as perspective-specific
  ordering, not silently promoted into one universal order.

## Open interpretation boundary

These pages establish distinct perspectives, components, chains, and orderings
but do not yet provide a complete executable grammar for assigning every
planetary landing to the Movement/Magnetic-Monopole frame. The live provider
therefore exposes its Movement stream rule as provisional and replaceable. It
must not be documented as resolved until an authoritative rule is supplied and
verified.
