# Primary-source grounding for modern MESSY

Read on 2026-10-08 from Sheldon Klein's Wisconsin archive, supplied by the user. This is a record of selected relevant passages and implementation consequences, not a claim of exhaustively reviewing the 92-page manual. Downloads and OCR are retained in the workspace at `/workspace/klein-sources`; URL, hash, and reviewed-page coverage are in `sources/klein-primary-source-manifest.json`. Original papers remain unchanged. Page references distinguish PDF pages from printed pages.

## What the sources establish

1. **MESSY already includes a generative rule loop.** The 1976 manual's foreword (PDF pp.1–2) describes behavioral rules and linguistic semantics using the same network notation. That network can generate simulation-language text compiled into new behavioral rules during a simulation, including rules describing how to derive new rules. Its term “text grammar” explicitly includes formal rules generating structured behavior. Modern game/app/media generation extends that idea with different output realizers.
2. **The historical universe is richer than triples alone.** The introduction (PDF p.8, printed pp.1–2) distinguishes the semantic network from other universe data, including class membership. Triples may contain two or three atoms; predicate nodes can point to lists of triples. Creation/deletion times, reality values, hypotheticality, groups, sequencing, and probabilistic subrules have separate meanings. They should not be collapsed into arbitrary geometry or display flags.
3. **Inference uses correspondence and corrective feedback.** Automatic Inference of Semantic Deep Structure Rules (1973 conference paper; this scan is a proceedings reprint), printed pp.567–571, PDF pp.12–16, connects semantic structures to both natural-language and simulation-language generation. It retains terminated facts with their times. Its Japanese hand-simulated example generalizes a rule, generates a test sentence, receives rejection, and refines the categories. This supports evidence-driven rule development, not treating plausible emitted code as verified behavior.
4. **I Ching relationships can transform a whole classification.** Analogy and Mysticism and the Structure of Culture (1983), printed pp.158–160, PDF pp.4–6, organizes 64 hexagrams into eight houses, describes ATO products mapping all eight members of one house to another, and shows a selected hexagram pair inducing a realignment of analogical relations. Terms are connected through named classification correspondences. This is more specific than attaching an arbitrary theme to a randomly chosen number.
5. **The same operation crosses output domains.** Analogical Foundations of Creativity (2002 revision), PDF pp.2–7, gives Boolean strong-equivalence, verbal, iconographic, decision-tree, and geometric interpretations. PDF pp.18–19 describes retargeting a plan through a common transformation and a hierarchy of operators over event sequences. Feature meanings and corresponding structure must be supplied; Boolean computation alone does not establish what a game mechanic or canonical quality means.
6. **Style and contact provide distinct forms of generation and learning.** Control of Style (1965), printed pp.619, 628–629, ties syntactic/vocabulary selection weights to source analysis and demonstrates changing pronoun and grammar weights. Language Contact Models (1974), printed pp.283–284, 286, models individual knowledge and contextual grammars evolving through interaction; input order can produce different generalizations. It explicitly extends the framework to semantic, social, and behavioral change.

## Consequences for this implementation

Reuse the existing `SemanticWorld`, AutoCoder, Boolean ATO core, and Klein tools. Preserve observed events, source identity, authored feature correspondences, and parent lineage. Distinguish candidate generation from execution evidence. Output producers can realize the same qualified structure as executable game behavior, app interactions, visual sequences, or other supported artifacts.

The user's full canonical address and morphological definitions govern admission and interpretation. The papers do not independently supply the user's Gate/Line/Color/Tone/Base mappings, nested dimensions, allowable crossing geometry, or Gemini's rendition. Their binary orientation and house classification must not silently become a King Wen gate mapping or a replacement canonical mesh. The connections to neural learning in the creativity paper do not supply a pretrained JavaScript code model.
