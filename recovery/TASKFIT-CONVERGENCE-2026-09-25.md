## Current implementation

`task-fit-capability.mjs` has a Computer mesh contract:
- `assess`: requires each upstream stage to be marked unresolved before capability assessment; a resolved stage returns its ownership immediately.
- Each task action reports observed direct ability, supported route, current evidence-backed constraint, or unknown. It names the reasons and evidence identifiers.
- Available support/adjacent/parallel/decomposed/delegated/complementary routes require evidence and are offered before a current inability status. Missing evidence remains unknown.
- Workable qualities require cited evidence. A numeric fit or tier is not issued.
- Supplied chart perspectives and derived natal, progressed, transit, cycle, geographic, asteroid, star, and HD data can be frozen with a hypothesis. The service stores the snapshot under a content hash and appends observed events to a separate science log. It does not invent chart calculations or interpret the chart on its own.

The caller currently supplies upstream resolution decisions, task actions, capability observations, route availability, and chart data. Automatic upstream orchestration and chart derivation remain open integration work.
